    Module three-text-geometry/tsl

    TSL node materials, published as the three-text-geometry/tsl subpath.

    They import three/webgpu and three/tsl, so they are kept out of the root entry: an app that only imports TextGeometry, the parsers or the R3F helper must not pull the WebGPU renderer into its bundle.
